FORMULA

X = F₀ / √[(k − mω²)² + (cω)²]

Assumptions

Required: a linear spring, viscous damping, and a direct force F₀ cos(ωt) on one degree of freedom. Transients from initial conditions are excluded. With c = 0, the result is the harmonic particular-solution amplitude; free motion does not decay. Undamped resonance with F₀ > 0 has no finite steady amplitude. ω = 0 gives static deflection F₀/k.

Displayed values are floating-point approximations. Design safety factors and unprovided error contributions are not calculated.
